# Words that are written for one another: homophones, near-spellings and the
# typos that happen to be words.
#
# A dictionary cannot help with any of these -- "a reel problem" is spelled
# perfectly. What separates them is the company each keeps: "real" follows
# "a" and precedes "problem" thousands of times, and "reel" never does.
# tools/mkusage counts that company in a large body of edited English, and
# the app compares the counts for the word that was typed against the counts
# for the others on its line here.
#
# One set per line. A word may be in more than one. Nothing here is a rule:
# a set that the counts cannot tell apart simply never fires.
